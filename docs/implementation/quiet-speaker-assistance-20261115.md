# Quiet speaker assistance

The daily limit is an upper bound, not a quota. MoMo should understand the user's life and help with concrete work, without turning every person encountered into a labeling task. This updates the interaction policy introduced in PR #76.

Speaker candidates now default to quiet accumulation (`importance=incidental`). To request attention the existing model must explain a concrete, short `benefit` and identify either a real current task (`task`) or a useful recurring person (`recurring`, backed by recording sources on at least two dates). Clear names or self-introductions alone do not justify a question or a separate people page. The model judges semantic relevance; the runtime validates scope, source availability, cross-day support and the interruption budget.

The shared selector prefers current-task needs to recurring-person candidates. It selects one person, enforces a rolling 24-hour maximum, ignores candidates older than seven days and suppresses recently asked names or overlapping recording selections for 30 days. Name normalization only suppresses questions; it never merges people or authorizes label propagation. Old unclassified proposals stay quiet until reassessed.

An unanswered delivered question pauses further proactive identity questions, even if that question's underlying recording changes. The user can leave it unanswered without another person being queued for tomorrow. The decline/confirmation route also resolves obsolete questions. Delivery selection and marking are serialized by the context-store transaction, and chat receipts remain idempotent. No extra push is emitted.

The question shows a source link, proposed name, concise practical benefit and exact labeling scope. The full model rationale remains available in the proposal record rather than being dumped into chat. Dream, worker tools and master CLI share the same fields and budget; instructions prohibit separate candidate lists or duplicate identity questions in ordinary replies and briefings. Explicit confirmation remains necessary before labels or phone voiceprint updates are applied.

Validation includes 100 incidental candidates producing no question; cross-day support; quiet candidate promotion; task priority; strict 24-hour spacing; ignored and obsolete unanswered questions; same-person/different-recording suppression; expiry; authenticated consent and replay. The real-model synthetic Dream test still corrects all seven tested personal-context errors/unknowns and preserves traceable notes, while leaving the one-off speaker unasked and unlabeled. 139 Python tests and 12 SuperAgent tests passed, along with TypeScript typecheck and the runtime bundle build. See [the acceptance JSON](quiet-speaker-assistance-20261115.acceptance.json). These are synthetic/runtime checks, not a physical-device or real-user interruption-frequency study. The running app has not been updated.
