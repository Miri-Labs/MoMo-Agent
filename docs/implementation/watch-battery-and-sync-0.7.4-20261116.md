# Watch battery drain and sync backlog repair — 0.7.4

## Root-cause evidence from dual-device telemetry

54 hours of telemetry from the physical Apple Watch SE (`00008301-1895081201B8202E`, `watchOS 26.6.1`) and receive ledgers from the paired iPhone (`00008150-000A31AA1447801C`) isolated why Watch battery drain spiked and why 10+ hours of new recordings remained stuck in the Watch queue:

1. **Pure capture is low-power; radio/retry churn caused the drain.** Continuous native capture plus 24 kbps AAC encoding used `2.1%–2.4%` CPU (`72–89s/h`) and `-5%/h` battery (`11-15 05:00–09:00`). Off-charger drain reached `-40%` in 50 minutes (`11-15 20:00–20:54`, CPU `76%–94.2%`) only after file-transfer failure loops started.
2. **Receipt starvation caused head-of-line re-upload deadlock.** At `11-16 11:31`, Watch reported `pendingChunks = 2,621`, while iPhone `sync-receive-diagnostic.json` already held all `1,440` chunks of Nov 14, all `1,440` chunks of Nov 15, and `605` chunks of Nov 16, with `pendingReceiptCountsBySource = 2,534` waiting to be acknowledged back to Watch.
   - Watch sent `receiptApplied` over interactive `WCSession.sendMessage` (`allowInteractive: true`), colliding (`WCErrorDomain 7007`) with `ReconcileRequest` and `AudioBlock` and stuffing 21+ background `transferUserInfo` slots.
   - `LinkSubmissionState.initialReceiptWaitDelay` was `120s`, shorter than a single multi-file BLE queue round (`11–17m`). `WatchRuntime.submitFileBatch` wrapped `fileCursor` back to `0` and endlessly re-uploaded the `2,534` already-delivered old chunks (`1,144` completions for `330` chunks in 28m; `5,643` completions in 4h overnight), starving newly sealed tail recordings (`rowid > 2534`) for 10+ hours. `submitAudioBlockCopy` also hardcoded `cursor = 0`.
3. **Premature stall cancellation + missing channel backoff caused `358,844` failed admissions and `watchconnectivityd` Jetsam.**
   - `FileAttemptLedger.requiredStallLease` evaluated `min(45, 90) == 45`, applying the 45s active-transfer stall lease to `completedUnits == 0` queued files waiting behind an active head-of-line transfer.
   - Heavy 12-slot `transferFile` churn and cancellations drove `watchconnectivityd` (`wcd`) into a memory `highwater` Jetsam kill (`JetsamEvent-2026-11-16-135856.ips`, `wcd` 192.6s CPU, `MoMoAgentWatch` 1,009.7s CPU). Without file-channel backoff, Watch cycled `2,500+` chunks at `8.2` failed admissions/s (`29,511/h`), synchronously rewriting `file-transfer-attempts.json` on each attempt.
4. **Non-Wi-Fi IP advertisement broke `LocalSync` LAN rescue and woke Watch Wi-Fi every ~17s.**
   - `LocalSyncServer.activeLANAddresses()` matched `name.hasPrefix("en")`, advertising non-Wi-Fi `http://10.10.13.158:58911` to Watch on a `192.168.2.x` Wi-Fi network.
   - Watch forced `requiredInterfaceType = .wifi` every `12s`, timed out after `5s`, recursively called `await reconcile()`, and left `DirectHTTPConnectionPool` Wi-Fi sockets open.

## Changes

- **Tail-first fresh chunk priority (`WatchRuntime.swift`):** `submitFileBatch` advances a dedicated `freshFileCursor` ahead of wrapped receipt-wait replays so newly sealed recordings upload immediately instead of waiting behind unacknowledged historical backlog; `submitAudioBlockCopy` skips chunks already in flight or awaiting receipts.
- **Receipt & control-lane isolation (`WatchRuntime.swift`, `RecordingStore.swift`):** `receiptApplied` uses background delivery (`allowInteractive: false`) and yields when `outstandingControlCount >= 8`, keeping interactive `sendMessage` clear for `ReconcileRequest` and `AudioBlock`. Indexed `receipts(delivered)` and `receipts(application_id)` eliminate full-table `json_extract` scans.
- **File channel backoff, receipt horizon & coalesced ledger persistence (`LinkTransport.swift`, `FileAttemptLedger.swift`, `WatchConnectivityTransport.swift`):** Added exponential file-channel backoff (`30s..300s`) with single half-open probe on channel failure; raised `initialReceiptWaitDelay` to `900s` (`max 3,600s`); lowered `bulkMaximumOutstandingFiles` from `12` to `6`; fixed zero-progress vs active-progress stall leases and suppressed zero-progress cancellation while head-of-line transfer advances; coalesced `transferFileResult` attempt-ledger writes (`250ms`).
- **Wi-Fi `LocalSync` & off-screen wake discipline (`LocalSyncServer.swift`, `WatchRuntime.swift`):** Restricted iOS LAN advertisement to `en0` with an active `.wifi` path (`NWPathMonitor`); gated unattended Watch Wi-Fi wakeups to real bulk backlogs (`pendingChunks >= 6` or `pendingReceipts >= 32`), applied `LinkRetryBackoff` (`30s..300s`) without recursive `reconcile()`, closed `.wifi` pool sockets on exit, stopped idle `scheduleAutomaticRecovery()` timer spin when file slots are full, and throttled off-screen widget status refreshes to `120s`.
